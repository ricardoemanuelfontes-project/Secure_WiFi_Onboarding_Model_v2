# Roadmap – Secure Wi-Fi Onboarding Model

## Phase 1 – Incremental Firmware Improvement
- Disable persistent onboarding mechanisms by default
- Introduce time-limited authorization window
- Add minimal logging and transparency
- Maintain backward compatibility

## Phase 2 – Secure Native Onboarding Architecture
- Integrate app-based explicit authorization
- Remove short reusable secrets from onboarding flow
- Enforce ephemeral credential generation
- Align hardware roadmap with secure-by-design principles

## Phase 3 – Long-Term Ecosystem Alignment
- Regulatory alignment
- Consumer transparency improvements
- ISP operational optimization
- Gradual deprecation of legacy onboarding methods

Goal: Risk minimization through proportional and economically viable steps.
